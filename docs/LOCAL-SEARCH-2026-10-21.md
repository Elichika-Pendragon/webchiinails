# Local search targeting — 21 October 2026

The website targets the actual Chii Nails studio in Palembang. Home now has a descriptive local H1 and the title “Salon Nail Art Palembang | Chii Nails”. Services covers French nails, custom nail art, extensions and basic manicure. Custom Sets covers portfolio, prices and press-on orders; Visit covers the real address, hours and directions. Natural wording variations are distributed by page purpose rather than repeated in a keyword list or duplicate city pages.

Existing static HTML, unique descriptions, production canonicals, sitemap, indexability guards, actual NailSalon address/coordinates/contact/hours and internal service/price links remain in place. There are no invented reviews, ratings, awards or neighborhood claims. The booking FAQ now describes the actual consultation → form/payment proof → customer-sent WhatsApp request → Chii approval flow. Private booking/approval pages stay noindex and outside the sitemap.

Owner photos have descriptive alternatives, responsive exports and a JPEG social fallback. The original brand artwork supplies stable PNG/ICO favicons and an Apple touch icon. Shared metadata names Chii Nails; WebSite supplies Chii Nails as the preferred name and Chii Nails Palembang as an alternative. See [brand search identity](BRAND-SEARCH-IDENTITY-2026-10-21.md) and [photo selection](JESSICA-PHOTOS-2026-10-21.md).

These changes improve the information supplied to crawlers; they do not establish rankings or guarantee first position. Google selects search names, icons and titles and must recrawl/process changes. A favicon or alternate name is not guaranteed to appear. No Search Console indexing request, live ranking measurement, Google Business Profile edit, review request, new domain purchase or paid SEO action was performed in this release.

Local Maps results also depend on relevance, distance and prominence. The separate Business Profile needs accurate verified business information, genuine customer reviews and current owner photos; the website cannot force “terdekat” placement for every searcher. No unsupported FAQ rich-result claim or FAQPage feature was added.
