# Printable customer receipt

Mode: Operate. Narrow confirmed request inside Chii's existing visual system; code-led, no concept tournament or generated imagery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make a verified payment easy to read, print and save without collecting PDF attachments. A receipt is a document, not a marketing page.

OWN-WORLD: Reuse the supplied Chii logo, DM Sans, white paper, blush screen surround and restrained rose emphasis. No additional fonts, raster assets, gradients or document dependencies.

STORY: Jessica accepts after checking funds, then sends the customer's own signed receipt link through wa.me. The customer reads the agreed service/time/amounts and explicitly prints or saves a PDF.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo and title, receipt number and DP/Lunas status lead into customer/date/time. A compact service row and aligned total/received/balance follow. One print action sits below the document.

FORM: A single-column receipt sheet is the specified structure. Preserve the payment form entirely. Public five-character order references remain optional; customers submit without an invitation, and owner approval remains separate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

The logo is the pre-existing owner-supplied export documented in DESIGN.md. No raster is created or replaced by this feature. Review evidence uses synthetic customer data and no real WhatsApp sends.

Current payment extension: sending is always optional. Explicitly confirmed balance receipt adds one audit event and updates the same customer link to Lunas. A cancelled receipt shows cancellation and received funds, with no amount due or refund claim. Preserve the quiet existing document and owner summary.
